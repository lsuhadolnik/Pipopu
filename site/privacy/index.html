Privacy Policy

Effective November 28, 2026

This policy covers Pipopu on iPhone, iPad, and Windows, as well as this website. Pipopu is developed by Lovro Suhadolnik.

Device discovery and transfers

Pipopu uses Bluetooth to find nearby devices and exchange the information needed to connect. The device name you choose is visible to nearby Pipopu users while your device is discoverable. Pipopu may use your local network to move larger files.

Transfer contents are encrypted on the sending device and decrypted on the receiving device. Pipopu has no account of its own and does not receive the contents of your transfers on a Pipopu server. The app does not include advertising or analytics.

Optional OneDrive features

If you sign in to OneDrive on Windows, Pipopu uses your Microsoft account to support cloud transfers or save received files to a OneDrive folder you select. Cloud transfers use temporary encrypted copies in your OneDrive. Pipopu deletes those copies when it can reach OneDrive after a transfer. If cleanup fails, you can delete them in OneDrive.

Files you choose to save to OneDrive are ordinary files in your account and remain there until you delete them. Microsoft processes your use of OneDrive under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

What stays on your devices

Pipopu keeps settings, device keys, and trusted-device records on your devices to recognize them on later transfers. On iPhone and iPad, Activity keeps local copies of transfer text and files. On Windows, local diagnostics record transfer stages and errors. They omit transfer contents, file names, and sign-in codes.

You can remove a transfer from Activity, remove a pairing in Settings → Trusted devices, sign out of OneDrive, and delete saved files or local Windows logs from their folders. Anything you copy from a received transfer goes onto your device’s clipboard.

Website and support

This website uses no advertising cookies or analytics scripts. The Windows app checks this site or GitHub Releases for updates. The hosting provider may process connection information, such as your IP address, to serve and secure the site.

If you contact support, we use your email address and the details you provide to handle your request. We keep correspondence for as long as needed to respond and handle any related follow-up or recordkeeping obligations. For privacy questions or requests to access or delete information you sent us, email lovro@solutions.si.
