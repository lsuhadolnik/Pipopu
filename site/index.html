How it works

From one screen to the other.

Choose what to send

Tap Send via Pipopu when sharing from your iPhone or iPad. On Windows, drop in a file or paste a link into Pipopu.

Pick your nearby device

Open Pipopu on the other device, then pick it from the list of nearby devices.

Pick up where you left off

Save the clip and drop it into your editor, or copy the link and open it on the bigger screen.

Security

Encrypted from send to receive.

Check who’s on the other end
On the first transfer, compare the six-digit code and approve it on both screens. Pipopu remembers the devices you trust. You can remove them later in Settings.
Keep files private in transit
Pipopu encrypts the contents on the sending device and decrypts them on the receiving device. That also applies to larger files sent through the optional cloud connection.
Send passwords without a preview
Copy a password or recovery code, then choose Send securely. Pipopu hides the text on both screens and skips the cloud queue. On the receiving device, select Copy secure text to put it on your clipboard.
