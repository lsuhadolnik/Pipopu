Pipopu Support

Need help with a transfer, or have a suggestion? Email Lovro Suhadolnik at lovro@solutions.si.

Your device is missing

Open Pipopu on both devices, keep them nearby, and check that Bluetooth is on. On your iPhone or iPad, enable Available to receive in Pipopu when sending from Windows.

A large transfer is stuck

Check that the devices are on the same local network. On your iPhone or iPad, allow local network access for Pipopu in Settings. If Windows asks whether Pipopu can use your private network, allow it and try the transfer again.

Pair your devices again

Remove the old pairing in Settings → Trusted devices on both devices. Send an item again and approve the connection once the six-digit codes match.

What to include in a support email

Tell us the app version from Settings, what devices you’re using, which direction you were sending, and what happened. Leave out passwords, pairing codes, and the contents of your files or clipboard.
