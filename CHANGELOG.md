# Pipopu 0.4.0

## Windows

- Open a file in Pipopu from Explorer after moving the app to Local AppData.
- Attachments hide the message field and clear after a successful send.
- Send clipboard text securely from the composer. Open Settings from the Send menu.
- The single-file app downloads updates in the background and restarts to apply them after ten idle minutes.

## iPhone and iPad

- Activity keeps transfers in conversations by device, with tap-to-copy text and access to saved files.
- An incoming transfer banner shows progress and opens the relevant conversation.
- The Share Sheet lists trusted devices when they are out of range.

## Website

- Added a dark design, Windows download steps, and an explanation of Bluetooth LE, local transfers, and OneDrive.
